# Put it on your phone (no coding)

You do this ONCE. After that you just open the app icon and paste a link.

## Part 1 - Put it online (about 10 minutes, free)
1. Create a free account at github.com and a free account at render.com (sign in with GitHub).
2. On GitHub: New repository -> name it video-to-text -> Create.
3. Click "uploading an existing file", drag in ALL files from this folder, press Commit.
4. On Render: New -> Blueprint -> pick your video-to-text repository -> Apply.
5. Wait about 3 minutes. Render shows a web address like https://video-to-text.onrender.com - that is your app.

## Part 2 - Install on your phone
- Android (Chrome): open the address -> menu (3 dots) -> "Install app".
- iPhone (Safari): open the address -> Share button -> "Add to Home Screen".

## Using it
- Open the icon, tap the box (it auto-fills if you copied a YouTube link), press Extract.
- Android bonus: in the YouTube app tap Share -> "Video to Text" and it extracts instantly.

## Good to know
- Free Render apps sleep when unused; the first open can take ~30 seconds.
- The hosted version reads existing captions only. Videos with no captions need the AI mode, which needs a stronger computer (see README.md).
- YouTube sometimes blocks cloud servers. If you always see "Could not read this video", tell me and I will add a workaround.
